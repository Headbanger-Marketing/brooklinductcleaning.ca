Setting limits on the rooms entered and photos taken during a visit

Unless a request mentions it, a provider cannot know to keep out of a room that holds papers, valuables or a child's belongings, and a visit can reach more of the home than the job needs. List each room of your Brooklin home with two details beside it: its status as enter, enter with notice or do not enter, and the registers in it. A room left out may limit what a written scope can cover, so ask the provider to say which registers that affects.

  1. Return grilles

    Where room air is drawn back toward the furnace.

  2. Return ducts

    The runs leading from each grille to the furnace.

  3. Furnace blower and coil

    The blower, evaporator coil and cabinet.

  4. Supply trunk

    The largest duct carrying air away from the furnace.

  5. Branch runs

    Smaller ducts feeding each room.

  6. Supply registers

    Where air comes back into each room.

Separate runs to ask about:Dryer ventHRV core and screensBathroom fan ductsRange hood exhaust

  • Photo rules to state

    Ask who keeps the photos, for how long, and whether they may be passed to anyone else.

Questions About Your Brooklin Request

Does Brooklin Duct Cleaning send its own crew to do the duct cleaning?

Any cleaning would be the work of a provider who responds, not of this site, and that provider must identify themselves and confirm they cover your address before any visit is arranged. This is an enquiry website, not a contractor, and it has no office, crew or vehicles of its own. It does not clean anything. The site cannot say who will respond. Our enquiry team reviews each Brooklin request and may forward it to a provider.

Have I agreed to anything by sending a request to Brooklin Duct Cleaning?

A request does not arrange a visit or set a price, and it neither names a provider nor confirms coverage at your address. What it does is put your description in front of our enquiry team for review. Any provider who responds must identify themselves, and nothing is arranged until you have agreed the scope, pricing and timing with them directly.

Can I send a Brooklin request that lists rooms the provider must not enter?

Yes. Describe each room with its status: enter, enter with notice or do not enter. Our enquiry team reads the list before any request may be passed on. A responding provider must then say in writing whether the work can be scoped around those rooms, and which registers or ducts an excluded room would leave out of the job.

May the provider photograph my home office or a child's room during the visit?

Only if you agree. State in the request which rooms may be entered and which may not be photographed at all, and ask the provider to confirm those limits in the written scope. A room you exclude from entry may mean that its registers or ducts are not cleaned, so ask the provider to list what that leaves out.

Who keeps the photographs taken in my Brooklin home, and who may see them?

Ask before any visit. The provider should say in writing who keeps the pictures, where they are stored, for what purpose they are used and whether any of them may be shared beyond the job. If you are not comfortable with an answer, narrow the photo rules in the written scope or decline that provider.

What if household members disagree about which Brooklin rooms stay closed?

Settle it before you send the request, because a provider can only work from one set of instructions. Name the person who can authorize work and who will be present when rooms are entered. If the room list changes after a quote, ask that the written scope be updated and agreed again before anything proceeds.

Can I leave a room out of the request and still get a complete duct cleaning?

That depends on the system and on what the provider confirms. Ducts that run through or serve an excluded room may be only partly reachable, so a quote can cover fewer registers than the whole house. Ask the provider to state in writing which parts are included, which are not, and whether any price changes because of the limits.

What should a Brooklin room list include when I request a dryer vent or HRV visit?

Include the laundry area, the rooms the vent passes through, the room holding the unit and the bathrooms or bedrooms its grilles serve. Give each a status and say whether the connector, hoods or unit may be photographed. These are separate from the furnace ducts, so mention each one even when the request is mainly about registers.
